# Third-party dependencies

Nava source and product attribution: Amin Fathi, 2026. No OpenAI endorsement or clinical society approval is implied.

Dependencies are resolved by Gradle and are not redistributed in the source archive except the standard Gradle wrapper. Consult the upstream licenses and notices when packaging a release:

- [Kotlin](https://github.com/JetBrains/kotlin)
- [Gradle](https://github.com/gradle/gradle)
- [AndroidX / Compose](https://android.googlesource.com/platform/frameworks/support/)
- [SQLCipher for Android](https://github.com/sqlcipher/sqlcipher-android) and its bundled cryptographic dependencies
- [OkHttp](https://github.com/square/okhttp) / [Okio](https://github.com/square/okio)
- [Kotlin coroutines](https://github.com/Kotlin/kotlinx.coroutines) and [serialization](https://github.com/Kotlin/kotlinx.serialization)
- [JUnit 4](https://github.com/junit-team/junit4) and [JUnit 5](https://github.com/junit-team/junit5), test-only

This register is not a complete audited SBOM. Generate a full dependency/license inventory from the final signed release build before distribution.
